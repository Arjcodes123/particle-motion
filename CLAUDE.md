## Project

Marketing site for Paramount Content Services, an SEO/AEO/GEO content agency.
Source briefs: `docs/*.docx` (the v2 strategy brief is operative).

**Read `docs/DECISIONS.md` before changing the hero, the colour tokens, the
motion primitives, or anything touching contact details / case-study data.**
Several non-obvious constraints are recorded there, including one hard rule:

> No content lives in the WebGL canvas. Every heading and all body copy stay in
> real DOM, because this client sells AI-search visibility and canvas pixels
> are invisible to crawlers.

## House style

Do not use em dashes in code comments, documentation, or user-facing copy.
Use commas, colons, semicolons, parentheses, or separate sentences instead.
